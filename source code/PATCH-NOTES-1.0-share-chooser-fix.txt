Harley's Clan Forum v1.0 — Share Chooser Cleanup
================================================

Internal version code: 10000014

Fixed
-----
- Share forum page no longer blindly lists every ACTION_SEND handler.
- Browser, Bluetooth and download/document handlers are excluded from the forum-link share chooser.
- Multiple activities from the same app are collapsed to one app row.
- Messaging/email apps are shown first, followed by social apps and other compatible share apps.
- Open in Browser continues to use the full external-app chooser and is not affected.
- Auto remains the default performance profile.
